# DigiStamp project guidance

DigiStamp is a mobile-first web app for collecting digital stamps and rewards. Design its screens as app interfaces, including the welcome screen before authentication.

- Keep onboarding in a compact single column on mobile and desktop. Avoid marketing-site hero layouts, oversized headlines, and decorative sample dashboards.
- Use the brand palette: dark brown #622B14, caramel #995F2F, olive #978F66, and cream #E4D6A9. Use neutral surfaces for readability and the palette selectively for branding, actions, and accents.
- The welcome screen includes a slightly tilted, perforated paper stamp card in the earth-tone palette. Use Manrope for its app text and controls, and Fraunces for the DigiStamp wordmark.
- Keep sign-in and account creation easy to find, with touch targets of at least 44px and visible keyboard focus.
- Preserve existing Supabase authentication and shop query destinations when adjusting the UI.
- Check the production build, lint, and rendered mobile layout after UI changes.
